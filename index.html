About

I'm a Senior Applied AI Scientist at Judi Health, where I work on automating prior authorization (PA). I use LLMs to cut down the manual review that slows patients' access to care.

I've spent 6+ years building NLP and LLM systems that ship to production. At CodaMetrix, I automated medical coding with fine-tuned BERT and Llama models and clinical information extraction. At Orbita, I led a generative AI virtual assistant that went to 10+ healthcare organizations. Before that, I built semi-supervised NLU models at Amazon Alexa.

I studied Computer Science (BS and MS) at UMass Amherst, where I did research in Andrew McCallum's IESL lab. Outside work, I play basketball and football, hike, bike, and travel whenever I can.
